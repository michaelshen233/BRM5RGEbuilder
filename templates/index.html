FROM YOUR WORKBOOK TO YOUR WORLD

Field guide.

The structure behind your commands.

A familiar workflow

  1. Choose a command and its world number.
  2. Enter the object UID or its %alias.
  3. Paste coordinates and adjust individual values.
  4. Save, arrange, then copy into RGE.

This website creates and records command text. It does not connect to Roblox or record gameplay.

Your data stays yours

Projects and up to 30 revisions per command are stored in this browser. Use Export backup regularly; clearing browser data removes saved work.

Import .xlsx to extract commands, .txt for one command per line, or .json to restore a backup. Imports create new projects and keep existing projects.

Workbook uploads are processed in memory by the Python server. Formulas are never executed; saved formula results are used.

Precision & uncertain syntax

Numbers are stored as text, preserving precision and -0. Comma-separated coordinates are accepted in the paste tool.

Unrecognized syntax is kept as a custom command and marked for review. Validation checks the supported structure, not whether a target or asset exists in your game.

Project maps show relative X/Z positions, not the Roblox terrain. Different worlds share the same schematic.

Workbook conventions

World numbers are positive integers. Explosion fields are ordered power, radius, X, Y, Z, type. Type suggestions preserve the workbook's spelling: Motar, C4, Flash, HelicopterVehicle.

Extra command details and deployment instructions are included in the project README.
